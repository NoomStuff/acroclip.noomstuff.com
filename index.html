I made AcroClip because a friend wanted a Medal alternative that doesn't train AI on your clips or shove ads in your face.

NoomStuff

Clip it

Capture what just happened so you never miss a moment.

Pick any screen or a window, or set the source to Auto and AcroClip picks up your game when it starts.

Clip with AltCor whatever you prefer

Manage your Collection

Clips land in your library. Group them by game, tag them, sort them into folders, sift and serach through them however you like.

They're plain MP4s in a folder you pick, so they keep working without AcroClip.

Rewatch, Trim & Share

Quickly watch anything from your library with the built-in player.

Trim the clips to the parts you want, and then share them with others.

You can also create shareables; smaller versions of your clips that are easier to share.
